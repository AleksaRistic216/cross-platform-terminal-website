### ☐ Step 3 — List CPT on SaaSHub (15 minutes)

File: `2026-09-11-directory-listings.md`, section **2. SaaSHub**.

1. Make a free account at <https://www.saashub.com>. If you have an email address ending in
   `@crossplatformterminal.com`, use it — SaaSHub reviews those first.
2. Open <https://www.saashub.com/services/submit> and copy in the texts from the file.
3. Don't skip the competitors list. Without it, the listing goes to the back of the queue.
